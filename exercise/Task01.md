\## Business Context



An online store specializing in gardening products sells plants, seeds, tools, pots, fertilizers, and other items. The company has a database with information about products, categories, customers, orders, items sold, and suppliers.



The management team wants to develop an interactive dashboard to monitor inventory and support product restocking decisions. Currently, the operations staff have difficulty quickly identifying which products need attention, assessing whether available stock is sufficient relative to recorded sales, and prioritizing the items that deserve monitoring.  

The company expects the dashboard to help answer questions such as:

\- Which products are below the recommended stock level for restocking?

\- Which products have zero or very low stock?

\- Which products have the highest sales volume and deserve attention regarding availability?

\- Which categories concentrate the most products that need restocking?

\- What is the estimated value of available stock, considering the registered costs?

\- Which products should be monitored with priority by the management team?

\- Which suppliers are associated with the products that need restocking?



The solution must use the data provided by the professor and present information that helps the team understand the current inventory situation and make more informed decisions.  

It is important to consider that canceled orders should not be treated as effective sales and that discontinued products should not be automatically recommended for restocking. In addition, the database has no stock movement history or supplier delivery lead times, which limits some analyses.  

Management has defined the general objective of the dashboard but has not yet determined which indicators, charts, filters, tables, and features should make up the solution. These decisions should be explored during the activity.





\## Proposed Activity



Using the provided database and a generative artificial intelligence tool, explore possible solutions to the problem presented and develop a dashboard proposal that meets the company's needs.  

Use AI to brainstorm, investigate analysis possibilities, and discuss which information would be most useful for management. Critically evaluate the suggestions received and choose those that make sense for the business and for the available data.  

Your proposal must explain:

\- What the dashboard's objective will be and which needs it will address.

\- Which indicators and information will be presented and why they are relevant.

\- Which charts, tables, filters, and interactions will be needed.

\- How the information will be organized to facilitate data interpretation.

\- Which business rules should be considered, including the handling of canceled orders and discontinued products.

\- Which limitations of the database may affect the proposed analyses.



The choices must be justified based on the business context and the available information. It is not enough to automatically accept the AI's suggestions: it is necessary to evaluate whether they are pertinent and viable.
